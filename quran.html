Коран: перевод смыслов

All 114 surahs in a beginner-friendly order. For each surah you can hear Elmir Kuliev's Russian translation read by Khalyaf Dzhafarov, then the Saheeh International English translation read by Ibrahim Walk, and optionally the Tafsir as-Sa'di commentary in Russian. Every Play button plays the recording in the player at the bottom of the page. Longer commentaries are split into parts labelled by ayah range, and the player moves on by itself: by default Russian then English, surah after surah, which you can change in the player.

About the recordings. Both audio sets come from Musulmanin.com, published by the Ummah publishing house. The commentary is the full Tafsir as-Sa'di, a widely used classical Sunni commentary; the site doesn't name its narrator. Al-Fatiha and Al-Baqarah also have an optional video tafsir as a second voice.
